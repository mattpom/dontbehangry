Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This cookie policy explains how Don't Be Hangry uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit dontbehangry.com.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit and improve your experience on return visits.

Cookies We Use

  • Analytics cookies: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site. This data is anonymized and aggregated — we cannot identify individual visitors.
  • Functional cookies: Used to remember your preferences and improve site functionality.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any cookies that identify you personally.

Third-Party Cookies

Google Analytics sets its own cookies to collect usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones — check your browser's help documentation for instructions.
