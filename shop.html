This Week's Kitchen Moves

  • Mise en place every time — Pre-measure and prep before you cook. The single habit that separates good cooks from great ones.
  • Cast iron over non-stick — Indestructible, oven-safe, and gets better the more you use it. A lifetime pan.
  • Instant-read thermometer — Stop guessing. A 15 dollar thermometer guarantees perfect chicken, steak, and bread every time.
  • Buy whole spices, grind fresh — Pre-ground spices lose potency in 6 months. A small grinder unlocks a different level of flavour.
  • Freeze herbs in olive oil — Ice cube trays filled with herbs and oil. Drop one straight into a hot pan for instant aromatic base.
