Every Dish Has a Story.

Food tastes better with context: where it came from, what it means and how to make the most of it.

Don't Be Hangry explores food through culture, history and practical advice. We ask who made a dish, how it changed and what it means to the people who cook it. Selected recipes and cooking guides give you a way to explore those ideas in your own kitchen.

We cover dishes, ingredients, markets and techniques, from the cultural role of dashi to everyday cooking questions. Some articles explain a food's history; others help you choose an ingredient or cook a dish. Every piece aims to make food easier to understand and enjoy.

The name is a joke with a point. Don't be hangry — don't let hunger make you eat without curiosity. The best meal you ever had was probably attached to a story. We're here for the story.
